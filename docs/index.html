Definition of done

Five concrete pieces of evidence, with limits stated clearly.

  • A clean command that reproduces the parser regression.
  • A trace of consecutive messages and error recovery.
  • A latency table with precise clock-edge boundaries.
  • Sisi’s board status: working evidence and blockers.
  • A short joint note choosing the next integration milestone.

Before you start: prerequisites & scope

This is a proposed Week 2 continuation of your Week 1 assignment, not a record of completed work. First reproduce the Week 1 parser demo, freeze the message format, and close any missing reset or basic decode checks. Use Days 1–5 as milestones if you are catching up.

Keep the existing Week 1 local contract unless you and Sisi explicitly revise it: a 32-bit application-payload stream in one clock domain, no input ready, low-byte-first lanes, final-byte mask and error indication, synchronous active-high reset, and an always-ready result consumer. The final verdict precedes a committed decoded result. The exercise’s 10 ns clock is a simulation setting, not measured board timing.

Do not connect the 64-bit FIFO directly to this 32-bit exercise interface without a documented width/boundary adapter. Decide whether buffering is needed after the continuous-stream behavior is understood. The hardware IP interface must be checked against the actual selected configuration.

Measure the payload-parser boundary this week. End-to-end Ethernet integration, wire-to-decision latency, architecture comparisons, and the finished research remain later milestones. Hardware loopback and synthesis are stretch work after the core regression.
